Job categories

Tag each job position with one or more categories (Operations, Programming, Year-round, Specialist, Bunk Staff, Leadership) — a job that fits more than one can have multiple tags. Used in the Analysis tab's Job Category picker.

Linked job names

Some jobs go by different names in different years (for example "Nurse" and "Camp Nurse"). Link them here and the Analysis tab treats them as one job. Your uploaded records keep their original names, and you can unlink at any time.

Staff Data

Any static fact about a staff member — home state, t-shirt size, whatever — tied to their Person ID, so it carries across every year they appear in. Edit cells directly, add a field, or upload a CSV to bulk-fill one or more fields.

Which column is the Person ID? Every other column becomes (or updates) a staff data field, named after its header.
